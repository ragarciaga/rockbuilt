# Idea: shift swap board for a small clinic

- 14 nurses, 3 shifts a day. Today swaps are agreed in a WhatsApp group and the head nurse copies
  them into a spreadsheet every Friday (about 2 hours a week, and swaps get lost).
- A nurse posts a shift she cannot cover; another nurse claims it; the head nurse approves.
- The head nurse must see the final roster for the week.
- Must work on phones. Personal data: names and phone numbers only.
- Budget: small. One part-time developer after launch.
